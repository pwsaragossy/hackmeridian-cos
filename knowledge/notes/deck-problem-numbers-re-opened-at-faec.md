# Deck problem numbers re-opened at origin 2026-10-25: DCSA 5.7, ICC 11.2, TT Club 22, NTC R 900M

[FACT, medium] The deck session re-opened these on 2026-10-25 around 17:20 UTC. It read the web pages through a summarising fetch tool. It downloaded the TT Club PDF and read it with pdftotext.
- NTC&Logística via Portal do Trânsito, 28 Mar 2026: "em 2025 foram registradas 8.570 ocorrências", "redução de 16,7%", "aproximadamente R$ 900 milhões".
- ICC, 21 Oct 2026: "The share of respondents using eBLs exclusively has risen to 11.2% in 2026", from 190 cleaned records.
- DCSA adoption guide: uptake "relatively low (5,7% in January 2025)".
- TT Club & BSI PDF: "Insider involvement accounted for 22% of global cargo" theft incidents.

Decision for the deck: slides 2 and 3 use only rows of PROBLEM-NUMBERS.md, so the deck, README, form and video quote the same numbers:
- 45M bills of lading a year;
- about 11% electronic in 2025;
- 22% of thefts with insiders;
- R$900M in Brazil;
- US$85M lost by HSBC in the Hin Leong case;
- US$2.5T trade finance gap (ADB).

DCSA gives two shares that conflict: 5.7% in January 2025 and about 11% as of 2025. The deck quotes about 11%, with its date, as [[dcsa-45-million-bills-of-lading-1af2]] advises. The ICC 11.2% figure was dropped, because two numbers near 11 with different meanings would confuse the reader ([[icc-fit-alliance-2026-ebl-survey-cdc7]]).
