# What Paulo's own documents and chat let the pitch say about the ZIS letter and videos

Six checked facts that the earlier notes ([[zis-rio-miami-context-from-paulo-4c79]], [[claims-hackmeridian-material-may-and-may-3e4f]]) do not hold, each with its source.

1. **Paulo's approval covers the Portuguese videos only.** Pedro sent two videos on 23/10 at 06:08; Paulo answered at 11:54 "Nada a alterar. Tudo a fazer." and asked for an English version. Pedro sent one English version on 23/10 at 23:00 and said he had changed a small moment about fundraising. The export shows no reply about it (next messages are the Workforce Brasil PDFs). "Paulo loved it" is Pedro's paraphrase; the words are "no change needed, everything to do". An earlier English pair went on 18/10 and got a sticker.
2. **The video's "Backed by" line (v2, 01:40-01:55) lists ADS, CISBRA, CAMACOL, the OAS, the chambers of Rio and Miami, Portal Comex Rio, Rocketbeet and Radox.** The first Whisper pass wrote "Ray Docks"; re-run unprompted with the small model it reads "Redox", so the last name is Radox (inference from sound, no caption on screen was checked). Pedro wrote that list himself. Paulo's own one-pager (RITLA, 17 Sep) says "the formal interest and authorized representation of each entity remain to be confirmed". So the line states more than Paulo's document does; only a signed letter can carry those names.
3. **The video hedges the bridge: "One possibility, giving every shipment a unique digital record..."** (v2 01:57). The bridge must keep "one possibility"; "the corridor needs exactly this" is not in any source.
4. **No Paulo document names Radox, Stellar, tokenization or a logistics section.** Glocal UpShift's deck says "Powered by Rocketbeet" and calls Rocketbeet the preparation layer; Radox as "technology partner at the same level as Rocketbeet" appears only in Pedro's draft letter. The RITLA page says "What was missing was the technology. That is no longer missing." (about the network platform, not shipment records). The chat's closest lines: "Vamos falar na volta em como vamos agir nessa construcao" (24/09) and "Eu comeco com o seu primeiro produto" (16/10).
5. **No Camacol president reaction exists in the export.** The only sponsor logo near that name is on the Workforce Brasil flyer (Colmaker, a different name). Any "the Camacol president loved it" needs its own source line before it goes in a deck.
6. **The letter has no commitment date from Paulo.** The "noon Sunday" and "27/10" dates are Pedro's. The draft was only partly sent (card, 2026-10-25), and Paulo calls the Workforce Brasil launch in Rio "nosso, 100% Corredor" (24/10) and the flyer puts it on Mon 26, 08:30-12:35 Rio time, so his Monday morning is taken.
