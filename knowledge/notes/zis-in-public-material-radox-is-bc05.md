# ZIS in public material: Radox is part of ZIS, but public text names ZIS only in Paulo's written wording

On 2026-10-25, Pedro said Radox is part of the ZIS project with Paulo Protásio, not outside support. This is a private fact, recorded on the board.

The independent reviewer re-checked the claims rules against this fact on 2026-10-25, with verdict "approve with changes". Pedro's statement alone does not allow any ZIS mention in public material, for three reasons:
- ZIS and Paulo are third parties.
- The e-mail to Paulo promises the material stays without the name if he does not answer.
- A judge cannot check the statement.

The cut-off for Paulo's written OK is Sun 25 Oct 18:00 Lisbon. The "27/10" date applies only to the Meridian talk.

**If the OK arrives by 18:00:** use the bridge sentence that names ZIS. If he signs the letter, add his signed text verbatim with his chosen title: "Radox, represented by its founder Pedro Wakigawa Saragossy, is part of the ZIS Rio–Miami project." (stronger wording asked of Paulo on Pedro's choice, 2026-10-25 ~12:50 UTC; quote only what he signs) Then PITCH.md line 19 becomes "No partner or client names, except ZIS Rio–Miami, named only in the wording Paulo approved in writing". Line 22 gains the ban: never "supports", "backs", "endorses", "validates", "ZIS pilot", or "part of the ZIS project" unless his signed text says it.

**If no OK arrives:** use the unnamed bridge ("a Rio–Miami trade-corridor initiative"), with no ZIS anywhere.

The phrase "part of the ZIS project" needs Paulo's own signature; Pedro chose to ask him for it. The reason Pedro gave for the title choice ("o piloto da ZIS") stays internal.
