# Founder statements in the deck: open conversations with ports, airports and chambers, and any-Stellar-asset access, wording and labels

1. Conversations. Allowed shape: "Per the founder (25 Oct): conversations are open with several ports, airports and chambers of commerce. None signed, none named, none confirmed." Not allowed: "partners", "in talks" (banned in check_deck), "working with", "doors open", "receiving our technology", any count above "several", any name.
