# HackMeridian rubric: 7 criteria and weights, scale 1-5 or 1-10 (sources conflict)

The HackMeridian build page lists seven criteria and says each is scored 1 to 5: Ecosystem Alignment (Scale; Track Alignment for Genesis) 20, User Value and Problem 20, Technical Execution 15, Innovation 15, Hackathon Progress 10, Market and Ecosystem Potential 10, Product Design and UX 10. They sum to 100. The page also gives a priority order Adoption, Discovery, Continuation without defining the words. For Scale the home page separately lists what is judged: milestone completion against the pre-event intake, SCF eligibility, production readiness, ecosystem impact. The portal tracks PDF has no weights; it only lists requirements. First fetched 2026-10-25 with WebFetch (a summary), then re-read directly as page text by the stage-1 reviewer the same day: scale 1-5 and the weights confirmed. The page is public, no login needed. Conflict: Pedro's verbatim paste of the portal rules page (2026-10-24, projects/hackmeridian-rail/PORTAL-PASTE.md) says the seven criteria "use a 1-10 rating scale", with the same weights. Until the live portal settles it, keep self-scores as a percentage of the maximum. The same page says thematic tracks are "Genesis Only" and do not apply to Scale.
