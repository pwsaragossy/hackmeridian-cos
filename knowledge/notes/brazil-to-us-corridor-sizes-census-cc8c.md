# Brazil to US corridor sizes: Census goods trade about US 94 billion in 2025, Vizion 525 thousand TEU Brazil to US 2024, Florida exports to Brazil US 6.1 billion

Researcher subagent, 2026-10-25 19:15 UTC. Not yet re-opened by the independent reviewer; MARKET-NUMBERS.md in hackmeridian-rail holds the reviewed version.
[FACT, medium; US Census Bureau, primary, read through a summarising fetch] US goods exports to Brazil US$49,012.9M (2024) and US$54,329.2M (2025); imports from Brazil US$42,313.9M (2024) and US$39,881.5M (2025). [INFERENCE] two-way goods trade about US$91.3B (2024) and US$94.2B (2025), all modes.
[FACT, low; Vizion is a container-tracking vendor blog, secondary, interested party] "In 2024, Brazil exported 525,054 TEU to the United States"; US to Brazil 342,588 TEU. Vizion's dollar values conflict with Census, so use only the TEU counts with attribution.
[FACT, medium; USTR, primary] "Florida exported $6.1 billion in goods to Brazil in 2025, representing 8 percent of the state's total goods exports."
[FACT, low; Container News, secondary] PortMiami FY2025 1,115,058 TEU (FY2024 1,089,443), no Brazil split.
