# Pearson gold case ended: court capped Air Canada at C$18.5k in Jan 2025, appeal settled Aug 2026

[FACT] The Brink's v Air Canada suit in [[three-lost-cargo-cases-for-the-cfc4]] is over. Federal Court (Judge Strickland), Jan 2025: Air Canada liable only up to the Montreal Convention cap, about C$18,500 (9,988 SDR), because Brink's did not declare the shipment's value; the ruling was about liability limits, not a finding of fault (Air Canada denied careless conduct). Brink's appealed; on 28 Aug 2026 Air Canada said the appeal was discontinued after a settlement, amount undisclosed. Criminal cases continue in Brampton. So a deck footer saying the claims are "not yet tested in court" is stale after Jan 2025. Do not put the C$18.5k on a slide: it is about an undeclared value, not about who held the goods. Also checked 26 Oct 2026: no report that the RTX 5090 cargo was found, and no airport or police statement found.
