# WTO 80 to 90 percent of world trade relies on trade finance, MLETR in 13 jurisdictions but not the US or Brazil, LAC trade finance gap US 350 billion

Researcher subagent, 2026-10-25 19:16 UTC. Not yet re-opened by the independent reviewer; MARKET-NUMBERS.md in hackmeridian-rail holds the reviewed version.
[FACT, medium; WTO, page undated, figure from the 2008-09 crisis era] "Some 80 to 90 per cent of world trade relies on trade finance (trade credit and insurance/guarantees), mostly of a short-term nature." Broad definition, not only documentary finance.
[FACT, medium; UNCITRAL status page, undated, latest entries 2025] 13 jurisdictions have MLETR-based or MLETR-influenced law (Bahrain, Belize, China, France, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Mauritius, Papua New Guinea, Paraguay, Singapore, Timor Leste, ADGM, UK). Neither the US nor Brazil is listed.
[FACT, low; MIGA press release, no method] "the worldwide trade finance gap currently stands at $1.5 trillion", "Latin America and the Caribbean accounting for $350 billion". Conflicts with ADB US$2.5T, see [[adb-trade-finance-gap-survey-dec-d033]].
[FACT, low; ICC, interested party] Trade Register "represents nearly a quarter of all global trade finance transactions" (29 Oct 2024); no public value totals. ICC DSI homepage: eBL could unlock US$30-40B trade growth and save US$6.5B (no method).
Not found: SWIFT MT700 volumes, ICC Trade Register value totals, a Brazil trade finance gap.
