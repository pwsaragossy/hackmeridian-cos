# HackMeridian 2026 official pitch deck template: cover plus 4 pitch steps

The organizers' template (PPTX, 5 slides, 1.07 MB, branded "HackMeridian 2026 · Lisbon", "#HackMeridian26") is a cover plus four pitch steps in this order: 01 The Solution (the answer to the pain point and why it beats other options), 02 PMF (despite the label, it asks for the problem: who is affected, how big, with numbers or a story), 03 Technical Workflow (how it works in practice, logic and feasibility over detail), 04 The Team (skills and roles, a small team photo, at most 3 lines per member). The file is the same template the rail PITCH.md cites as a Google Slides link, so the deck can be built straight from this PPTX; PPTX is an accepted deck format up to 20 MiB ([[hackmeridian-2026-rules-and-submission-requirement-5868]]).

The four steps do not cover what Scale asks of the deck (target users, value proposition, future vision, business model, market, go-to-market), so added slides stay needed. The template puts Solution before the problem; the rail PITCH.md says it keeps the template order but puts PMF (problem) first, so that line or the order must change. The team slide limit (3 lines per member, photo) applies to PITCH slide 9.
