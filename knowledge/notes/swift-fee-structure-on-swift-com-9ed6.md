# SWIFT fee structure on swift.com: one-time entry fee, annual charges with network connection, monthly network-usage traffic fees, no public amounts; 12500 institutions, 53 million FIN a day

Researcher subagent, 2026-10-25 ~19:25 UTC, pages read in a browser. The reviewer's verdict and the approved sentence are in MARKET-NUMBERS.md section 5 (hackmeridian-rail).
[FACT, medium; Swift billing rules page, undated, current FX rates] Three fee parts: "Entry fees are invoiced after receipt of the Swift Undertaking."; "Annual charges (including Swift BIC costs, network connection costs, software maintenance, documentation subscriptions) ... are invoiced in January of each year."; "the network usage part (message transmission traffic) of the invoice covers messages sent". Monthly billing. "priced in EUR only". No amounts on any public page (price list probably behind myswift login).
[FACT, medium; swift.com who-we-are, undated] "53 million+ FIN messages sent every day on average" (matches the 2024 review); "over 12,500 institutions across more than 200 countries and territories" (tile says 220+, so say 200+). Record day: "over 68 million messages exchanged in a single day" (30 Dec 2025).
[FACT; swift.com about-us] "a global cooperative owned by our members and governed by the world's central banks".
Not verified (Annual Review PDFs return 403): revenue split 2024 (traffic EUR 473.7m, recurring 402.7m of EUR 1,114m) and 2025 average 59.8m FIN a day, search summaries only.
Decision context: Pedro chose a pricing model of a subscription per connected institution plus a fee per shipment, after SWIFT, with no values (via the status session, 2026-10-25).
