# Which ZIS video versions Paulo saw, what changed between them, and the only Camacol-president trace

Checked by transcribing the video files themselves, not by reading the chat alone. Four versions exist; Paulo's "no change needed" covers only the pair sent on 23/10 at 06:08.

1. **Version of 10/10 (Portuguese, Rio-Miami).** Paulo answered with a sticker only. Its trust line says tokenization is a shared layer of trust where cargo is financed at the port of origin and paid on delivery, a company raises capital from investors in both countries, and avoided carbon becomes an unalterable record. Its backers line names ADS, CISBRA, CAMACOL, the OAS, the chambers of Rio and Miami, Portal Comex Rio and Rocketbeet, and does not name Radox. Its figures say Brazil and Florida trade about US$27 billion a year and Rio's exports to the US fell 26% in 2026.
2. **Version of 18/10 (English, Rio-Miami).** Same financing and carbon lines; the backers line now ends with Radox (read as "Redox" by Whisper small, "Ray Docks" by Whisper base). Radox was therefore added to the backers line by Pedro between 10/10 and 18/10. The export shows only a sticker back from Paulo. The Rio drop is now 11% and the trade figure US$27.5 billion in 2025, so Pedro's own versions disagree on that number: no figure in these videos is safe to reuse.
3. **Pair of 23/10 06:08 (Portuguese), the one Paulo answered "no change needed".** One of the two is the bioceanic video, which is on disk. Its bridge says "for example, giving every shipment a digital record that all four countries check at the same time... nothing can be erased without leaving a trace... that is what is called tokenization... the technology for this already exists." The other video of the pair is not on disk, so what the Rio-Miami Portuguese cut said (financing lines or not, Radox in the backers line or not) is unknown.
4. **English v2 of 23/10 23:00 (Rio-Miami).** Pedro cut the fundraising moment ("not in the script") and hedged the bridge to "one possibility". No reply from Paulo in the export. This is the file with the "Backed by ... Radox" line and the G20 line.

What follows for the pitch: the only bridge wording Paulo has certainly seen and answered is the bioceanic one (Portuguese), which is about the Rio to Antofagasta corridor, not Rio-Miami. The "one possibility" wording of Rio-Miami v2 is Pedro's later edit. The financing lines Pedro cut were the closest the videos came to the project's buyer (someone paying against goods in transit).

**Camacol president.** The Camacol president is Joe Chi per Pedro's own draft letter (source Miami Today 2020, still holding the post unconfirmed). The export holds one line from him, inside a message Paulo forwarded on 17/10: a request to translate the bioceanic communique into English for the Secretary General and his directors. That is a request about a text, made before the videos were sent, not a reaction to them. The same draft says there is no record of a direct conversation between Pedro and him about Radox. So "the president of Camacol received the videos well" has no source in the export, the brain or the PDFs; ask Pedro where he heard it.

**Nearest corridor-side source for the direction.** Paulo's Rio-Florida mission deck (24/09, from Portal ComexRio) has a Green Corridor slide listing "emissions traceability: transparency throughout the logistics chain". It is about emissions, not custody, and is not Paulo's own text, but it is a source that is not our own video.
