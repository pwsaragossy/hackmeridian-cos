# ADB trade finance gap survey Dec 2025 still says US 2.5 trillion, so cite the 2025 edition not the 2023 one

The Asian Development Bank page "ADB Global Trade Finance Gap Survey" (publication date December 2025, opened 2026-10-25 through a summarising fetch tool, so wording is second-hand) says the estimated global trade finance gap "remains at $2.5 trillion, as in 2023". The older edition, the 2023 Trade Finance Gaps, Growth, and Jobs Survey (September 2023), put the 2022 gap at US$2.5 trillion, up from US$1.7 trillion two years earlier. The number is the same; the edition is not.

Consequence for the Rio-Miami deck: slide 3 (problem) cites "US$2.5T global trade finance gap in 2022 (ADB, Sept 2023)". That edition is superseded. Cite the newest one: "US$2.5T global trade finance gap (ADB Global Trade Finance Gap Survey, December 2025)". The figure sizes the financing shortfall between requested and approved trade finance; it is context for the market, not evidence of the custody pain, and the slide must keep saying so. The edition year must match the sentence on the slide, so re-open the ADB page before the deck is exported.
