# DCSA eBL 5.7 percent Jan 2025, ICC DSI 36 key trade documents, Winson Oil forged bills of lading ruling 2023

[FACT, medium] DCSA (industry body of carriers, interested party): market uptake of the eBL is "relatively low (5,7% in January 2025)". Secondary only: Breakbulk reports 11% by August 2025 "according to data from the Digital Container Shipping Association" (not opened at DCSA).
[FACT, medium] ICC DSI, 24 April 2024: analysis covered "all 36 key trade documents"; "21 of 36 documents already have standardised electronic versions". This counts document types, NOT documents per shipment; the 240 copies / 30 parties figures were not traced to a primary source and are dropped.
[FACT, medium] GTR 21 Aug 2023: Singapore court found Winson Oil's representations to Standard Chartered and OCBC "were thus made fraudulent"; claim over US$60mn on two gasoil cargoes; Hin Leong estimated debts US$3.5bn (that is Hin Leong debt, not fraud loss). Press source, not the judgment.
McKinsey eBL figures (US$6.5bn): McKinsey site timed out, not opened, dropped.
