# Market slide 7 re-check 2026-10-26: DCSA 45M, ADB 2.5T, WTO 80-90 percent re-opened; SDF 4B counts treasuries and funds, not goods, so it leaves the market slide

Re-opened 2026-10-26 04:35 UTC through a summarising fetch tool (wording second-hand for ADB, SDF, WTO).
[FACT, medium] DCSA, 15 Feb 2023: "Ocean carriers issue around 45 million bills of lading a year." ADB, Dec 2025: the gap "remains at $2.5 trillion, as in 2023" (same-number edition, see [[adb-trade-finance-gap-survey-dec-d033]]). SDF page: "4 billion dollars in tokenized real-world assets, as of September 1, 2026"; the asset types it names are treasuries, funds and yield-bearing assets; the page does not mention trade finance, supply chain or goods (see [[stellar-tokenized-rwa-size-on-2026-a455]]). WTO trade finance page: "Some 80 to 90 per cent of world trade relies on trade finance (trade credit and insurance/guarantees)", page undated (it lists news to 16 Oct 2026). The WTO Global Trade Outlook PDF gtos0326 does not repeat the 80-90 figure for trade finance, so WTO page stays the single source.
[INFERENCE] The US$4B measures tokenized financial assets, not goods in transit: as a "market" number it invites the question "what has that to do with custody", so it leaves slide 7. The 45M x 4 = 180M handovers line (see [[market-numbers-reviewer-pass-corridor-teu-2a95]]) is the only bottom-up figure and is built on our own unit; the 4 is the demo's four handovers (rio-miami-rail README lines 50-54).
