# Stellar and Solana hackathon winners: no winning deck is public, only organizer rules and video guidance (checked 2026-10-25)

Searched web, Stellar blog, DoraHacks via Scout and Lumenloop on 2026-10-25 for slide-level winning decks (Meridian, Stellar Hacks, Sorobanathon, Colosseum, ETHGlobal, Chainlink). Not found in those places: any winning deck PDF. Winners are named without decks. HackMeridian 2025 (Rio): Open Innovation 1 The Simple Fund, 2 4Bridge, 3 Stellar Forge; Composability 1 Lance, 2 Star Lends, 3 Panorama Block; six finalists pitched live on the main stage; no judging notes. Sorobanathon 2023: 1 Cowchain Farm, 2 Divify, 3 Kwickbit+Paltalabs, all linked from Devpost. Stellar Hacks ZK Gaming 2026: 1 Chickenz.io, 2 xray.games, 3 Stellar Poker; each submission is a public repo, live URL and 2-3 min YouTube video, so the video and README are the real winning artifact. Organizer text is the evidence on what is rewarded: Stellar Hacks ZK says it wants an honest work in progress over a polished mystery, says mock data must be stated in the README, and wants the technology load-bearing, not only on a slide. Colosseum: the pitch video is first reviewed and decides shortlisting; max 3 minutes; order team, problem, user, validation (even informal), vision; a separate 2-3 min technical demo covers the how; with no users, say how you will get the first ones. Penalised: over time, flashy visuals with little substance, buzzwords, no team background, unclear chain integration. Interested parties: Colosseum and Chainlink are organizers, not neutral. Single-source on the slide order of winners: none; it is an inference from video guidance.
