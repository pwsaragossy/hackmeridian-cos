# ICC FIT Alliance 2026 eBL survey: 51.1 percent adoption but only 11.2 percent exclusive, so paper still runs beside it

[FACT, medium] ICC/FIT Alliance 2026 survey (190 cleaned records; survey of members, interested party: promotes eBL): "eBL adoption reached 51.1%", up from 49.2% (2024) and 33.0% (2022); exclusive eBL use 11.2% (7.5% in 2024); banks at least partial use 22.2%; 93.8% see eBL as enabler. Reading: about 9 in 10 adopters still run paper in parallel (dual-format 39.9% per search summary, not seen on the page). It is a share of respondents, not of bills.
