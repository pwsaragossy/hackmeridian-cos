# SCF Build participant rules: entity in good standing, sanctions screening, and what the page does not say

HackMeridian judges Scale entries on "SCF eligibility", and the README of the Rio-Miami rail has a Production path section for it. The SCF awards page says eligibility is set by the Official Rules in the SCF Handbook, and the Handbook page "Official Rules for Submissions" (read 2026-10-25 through a summarising fetch tool, so second-hand) holds the participant rules.

What that page says: a submitter is a team of eligible individuals or an organization "duly organized or incorporated and in good standing"; one Representative acts for the team; at least two eligible individuals must be available for webinars, bootcamp and a demo day if the project is a finalist; individuals are 18 or older, not residents of OFAC-sanctioned countries or regions, and not on the OFAC SDN list; the participant and its directors, officers and shareholders must not be sanctioned persons; SDF can require re-verification at any time and pauses payments until it is done (10 calendar days to complete). Excluded: unlawful products, projects that mainly promote a token without real-world use, use cases that do not contribute to Soroban or Stellar, and projects with pending or threatened litigation or regulatory action that could impair delivery.

What the page does not say (so nothing here may be claimed either way): an open-source requirement, a required testnet or MVP stage before applying, conflict-of-interest rules, rules for contractors, and the initial KYC or KYB process. The awards page lists the review criteria (product market fit, track-specific criteria, submission quality, use of Stellar, integration plan, ready to build, budget and tranches) and says payments come in tranches named MVP, Testnet and Mainnet. A semantic-search summary of the awards page (a candidate row with an unverified-match caveat, not read on the page itself) puts SCF Build round #46 as open from 2026-10-11 until 2026-11-08.

Consequence for the README: write what the rules require and which parts the team can show (the Soroban contract, a testnet milestone), and leave the entity, residency and sanctions facts to the company that would apply. Do not write "we are eligible". Whether the Radox company meets the entity rule is the owner's fact, not an agent's.
