# Founder voice in public text: sell the strong part, no self-deprecating caveats, nothing false

Write in the founder's voice. State strengths as facts. Give the roadmap with confidence, for example "We are building the end-to-end custody rail…".

Hard line, unchanged:
- Nothing false.
- No partner named.
- No signed deal.
- No users, customers or revenue claimed.

Required disclosures that stay: prior work, AI usage, testnet.

How we applied it on 2026-10-25 (README, FORM-ANSWERS, DEMO-SCRIPT, CUSTODY-SPEC):
- Removed: "neither the standard nor the rail exists yet", "these are market figures, not our traction", "no customer has confirmed it", "our prices are not set", "proves the mechanism, not independent custodians", "no one has adopted it", "only evidence, cannot be reproduced".
- "Radox is in conversations with ports, airports and chambers of commerce" (no names) is stated as fact. It is the founder's own statement ([[founder-statements-in-the-deck-open-89a2]]).

Where truth still needs care, rephrase instead of hedging:
- Where a claim rests on a teammate's word, say only what we checked. Example: "two teammates signed their own handovers with their own keys" is checked from the signatures. "keys that never left their computer" is not checked.
- When a capability ran only in a local run, say "running locally" and do not hedge further.
- Search results keep their scope: "No project in the Stellar directories we searched does dual-signed custody of physical goods".
- Market numbers stay attributed to their source. They are never called ours.
