# Deck and video rules for async hackathon judging, from Colosseum, Stellar Hacks and SCF guidance (2026-10-25)

Steps distilled from organizer guidance, applied to an async-judged deck. 1 Open with the problem and the user in the first 20 seconds, then team background. 2 Show the working product early, not last; cut dead time. 3 Put the chain integration on its own beat: what is on-chain and why that chain (Colosseum: judges care why the team chose it). 4 Show validation honestly: informal user feedback counts; with none, state how the first users arrive. 5 State mocks and gaps in the deck and README. 6 End on a next step with a date and a link. 7 Give judges every artifact: public repo, live URL, video. SCF guidance (2023-05-10): show Soroban/Stellar expertise, ecosystem impact against named competitors, a 3-month roadmap with deliverables and budget from engineering hours. Limits: all organizer-written; no neutral study of winners.
