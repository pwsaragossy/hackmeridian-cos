# UNCTAD 920 million TEU port throughput 2024, 12720 million tons seaborne trade, around 80 percent by sea, WTO US 26.26 trillion merchandise exports 2025

Researcher subagent, 2026-10-25 19:17 UTC. Not yet re-opened by the independent reviewer; MARKET-NUMBERS.md in hackmeridian-rail holds the reviewed version.
[FACT, medium; UNCTADstat, data updated 15 Apr 2026, read through a summarising fetch] "In 2024, 920 million twenty-foot equivalent units (TEUs) of containers were handled in ports worldwide." Port throughput counts every lift, so transshipment counts twice.
[FACT, high; UNCTAD Review of Maritime Transport 2025 ch. I, text read with pdftotext; data from Clarksons Research] "Maritime trade volumes reached 12,720 million tons in 2024, growing by 2.2 per cent".
[FACT, medium; UNCTAD topic page, sentence undated] "Around 80% of the volume of international trade in goods is carried by sea". The UNCTAD news page says "over 80%"; use "around 80%".
[FACT, high on text, source is a mirror] WTO Global Trade Outlook and Statistics, March 2026: "The current US dollar value of world merchandise trade as measured by exports was US$ 26.26 trillion in 2025, up 7% compared to 2024." Read on astrid-online.it; cite the wto.org copy.
Not verified: World Shipping Council "about 250 million containers" a year (secondary, WSC page 404).
