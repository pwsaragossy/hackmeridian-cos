# World Bank and IAPH port community system adoption: under 10 percent of low and middle income ports, 80 percent high income (2023)

Opened 2026-10-25. [FACT, medium] A World Bank manager (Sebastien Dessus) is quoted by IAPH's Sustainable World Ports page of 2023-11-01: "Over 90 percent of the ports in low- and middle-income countries have not yet implemented these PCS platforms." and "less than 10 percent of ports in low- and middle-income countries use Port Community Systems, compared with 80 percent in high-income nations"; sample "over 897 ports" from UNCTAD's LSCI, 201 countries. It is a conference remark relayed by an IAPH site, not the report text (the report PDF on openknowledge.worldbank.org returned 403 to me). The World Bank publication page (2023-11-01) says "just 16 percent" of ports using the platform are in low and middle-income countries. [FACT, high] IMO FAL.5/Circ.54 (2024-04-25), the IMO PCS guidelines, section 8.4.3 "Legacy System Integration": "When legacy systems with proprietary data formats and interfaces exist, at the early stages of the PCS introduction, some data format/interface conversion services should be considered." Not found: an IPCSA survey with a share of ports without a PCS (IPCSA site returned HTTP 500; search found none). Cost per port (small under 10 million USD, large over 100 million) appears only in trade press snippets I could not open: unverified, do not use.
