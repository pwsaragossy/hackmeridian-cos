# HackMeridian Code of Conduct play fair: build and demo only your own work or work you have the right to use

[FACT] The Participant Code of Conduct, Terms and Liability Waiver is public at app.hackmeridian.com/code-of-conduct (the portal sign-in page links it; the earlier note [[no-text-says-a-hackmeridian-scale-7074]] listed it as not read). Section 10 "Play fair" reads: "Build and demo only your own work or work you have the right to use. Credit any third-party code, tools, or assets. Follow the submission rules honestly and accept judging decisions graciously." Same section: the Organizer "may warn, remove, or disqualify anyone who breaks this Code, without refund or prize eligibility". Section 13: "You keep ownership of what you build ... Make sure you have the right to submit everything in your project."

[INFERENCE] This is the likeliest origin of the memory that "only" something is allowed: the word "only" is there, but it limits whose work (own or licensed), not when it was written. Nothing in it says new code only; it says nothing about existing code, a closed backend or a public/closed split. The test it sets for the repo is ownership and credit of third-party parts, plus honest following of the submission rules ([[hackmeridian-2026-rules-and-submission-requirement-5868]]: disclose prior work).

Not found in: the Code of Conduct sections 1 to 20, Terms sections 1 to 17 (checked by keyword search and full read of the Terms). Still not read: the logged-in portal rules page, the Privacy and GDPR Notice, "prize eligibility, judging and payout rules ... shared separately" (Code section 13), organizer announcements. No legal interpretation made here.
