# ADB Dec 2025: 41 percent of SME trade finance requests rejected, documentation is not a listed reason

[FACT, medium-high; ADB survey of banks] Figure 2 / text: "SME rejection rates (41%), for the first time, are reported to be close to those of mid-cap and corporate clients (40%)" (2023: SMEs 45%, large 30%). Gap: "By 2023, it had reached $2.5 trillion, and remains at that level in this 2025 survey". Top reasons banks reject (Fig 4): economic slowdown, unacceptable country or counterparty risk (top two for more than 47%); KYC concerns ranked first by just over 15%. Documentation or custody is NOT a listed reason; the survey "did not investigate the drivers of these changing rejection rates". So it cannot be used as proof that missing documents cause rejection. Rejection rate is by value or count not confirmed on the text I read.
