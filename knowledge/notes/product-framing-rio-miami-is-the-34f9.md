# Product framing: Rio-Miami is the first corridor pilot, the goal is a SWIFT-like standard for tokenized-asset rails

Pedro, 2026-10-25: "o piloto é rio miami, mas o objetivo disso é para criarmos um standard como o swift, mas para ativos tokenizados em rails no mundo todo, em parceria com as câmaras de comercio, aeroportos e portos, integrando com o sistema de todos eles."

How to use it:
- Rio–Miami is the first corridor. It is not the product. The product is Radox Custody Rail, a generic rail. This is why the title is "Radox Custody Rail" and the tagline is "First corridor: Rio–Miami".
- The long-term goal goes only in future-tense sections: the README Production path or Future section, the video's closing line, the portal "What's next" field, and the deck's last slide. Write it as a goal, for example "we aim to grow this into a shared standard for custody of tokenized goods across trade corridors, like SWIFT did for payment messages, working with chambers of commerce, ports and airports and integrating with their systems".
- Never write that a standard exists, that anyone has adopted it, or that a partnership exists. Name no chamber, airport or port.
- The present tense still covers only what ran: 7 testnet transactions with test keys ([[claims-hackmeridian-material-may-and-may-3e4f]], [[wording-the-rio-miami-deck-readme-ef38]]).
- The ZIS naming rule stays as it is ([[zis-in-public-material-radox-is-bc05]]).
