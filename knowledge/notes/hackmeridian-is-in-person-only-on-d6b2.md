# HackMeridian is in person only on the FAQ but the terms do not say every team member must attend

Read on 2026-10-25 through a summarising fetch tool, so wording is second-hand. The FAQ says "HackMeridian is in person only" and "There is no remote option". Terms section 02 places the Event in Lisbon on 25 to 26 October 2026, section 03 asks only for age 18 or more and says acceptance is at the organizers' discretion, and section 05 asks participants to follow venue rules and staff instructions. None of these pages says that every member of a team must be physically present, and none says what happens to a team with a remote member. Pedro's paste of the portal rules says nothing on presence either, and the portal dashboard asks each user "Will you be in Lisbon?" (quoted by an earlier session, not re-opened).

So the risk that a remote teammate disqualifies the team is a suspicion, not a found rule: the legal specialist's ranking of it as the top risk rests on the FAQ sentence alone. The cheap control is a fact only the owner has (are Leo and Vhendala both at Warehouse ONE16 on Sunday) and, if one is not, a question to an organizer at check-in before building starts.

Not checked: the portal event-rules page (needs login), the portal RSVP text, and whether the organizers apply the rule to teammates who only join the team in the portal.
