# ZIS Rio-Miami context from Paulo Protasio: RITLA, corridor launch Mon 26 Oct, letter not yet sent

Internal context for the pitch; partner material, not for public quoting without Paulo's consent ([[claims-hackmeridian-material-may-and-may-3e4f]]).

Status of the letter of participation, as the chat shows it: on 23/10 Pedro asked Paulo for a letter showing Radox's participation and the supporters, "para dar força na apresentação"; Paulo answered asking for a guiding text and a draft. Pedro replied he would write and send them when he arrived. The export (last message 24/10 21:59) shows no draft sent; the draft exists in the Radox brain (CARTA_PARTICIPACAO_ZIS_LISBOA_RASCUNHO_2026-10-24.md). Pedro expects it signed by Sun 25 noon. Until a signed copy exists, the deck uses the version without it ([[wording-the-rio-miami-deck-readme-ef38]]).

What Paulo has said in writing that bears on the pitch: on 23/10 he answered Pedro's two Portuguese explainer videos (Rio–Miami and bioceanic) with "Nada a alterar. Tudo a fazer." and asked for English; Pedro sent English v2 at 23:00 the same day and Paulo has not answered it ([[which-zis-video-versions-paulo-saw-1bb9]]); on 22/10 he confirmed the new corridor is "ZIS e GLOCAL"; on 24/10 he said the Workforce Brasil programme is "100% Corredor". He calls the Mon 26 Oct event in Rio "the beginning of the Rio-Miami Corridor", to be promoted with the southern and south-eastern state governments and the Florida government.

Context from his documents: RITLA is an intergovernmental organization set up in 1983 (Argentina, Brazil, Mexico, Nicaragua, Venezuela) with its nucleus in Rio; it proposed the International Services Zone (ZIS) at the UNCTAD regional conference in Rio in 2003; Rio-Miami is described as the first binational node of the ZIS, installed in 2026. The Workforce Brasil manifesto is dated Rio de Janeiro, 26 October 2026, the 43rd anniversary of RITLA, and lists three corridors (Miami-Rio, the Americas corridor articulated with the OAS, and the bioceanic corridor). None of these documents mentions tokenization, Stellar or Radox, so the link between the corridor and the custody contract comes only from Paulo's letter, once signed. The submission day (Mon 26 Oct) is also the corridor's launch day in Rio.

Pedro's two explainer videos for the ZIS (English, 1080p, local copies ~/Downloads/zis-rio-miami-axis-en-v2.mp4, 2:51, and ~/Downloads/zis-bioceanic-from-rio-to-the-pacific-en.mp4, 2:39; transcribed locally with Whisper base on 2026-10-25) are the English versions made after Paulo answered the Portuguese pair on 23/10; he has not answered the English ones. The English versions also answer Joe Chi's (Camacol president) 17/10 request, a request about a text forwarded by Paulo, not a reaction to a video (the first video went to Paulo on 10/10), to translate the corridor material "for the Secretary General and his directors". Both end on the same idea, which is what the hackathon builds: give every shipment a unique digital record that both sides (or all four countries) can check at the same time, every step recorded from origin to destination and nothing erased without a trace, "that is what we call tokenization", and "the technology for this already exists". The Rio-Miami video also says the axis arrives at the G20 in the United States in December 2026 "as evidence, not as a promise", and lists backers of the ZIS (ADS, CISBRA, CAMACOL, the OAS, the chambers of commerce of Rio and Miami, and others); those are the initiative's backers as the video states them, not endorsements of Radox or of this project. Its trade figures (Brazil as Florida's top trading partner, US$27.5 billion in 2025; Rio's exports to the US down 11% in 2025) and the bioceanic figures (17 days sooner to Asia, 30% lower freight, attributed to Brazil's foreign ministry) need their primary sources before any reuse.
