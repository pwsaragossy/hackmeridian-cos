# Where the letter titles and the Glocal laboratory wording come from, and what Paulo wrote about Radox

Four checked facts for the e-mail asking Paulo to sign a narrowed participation letter.

1. **Paulo's title in his own latest document is ADS, not CISBRA.** The Workforce Brasil programme he sent on 24/10 lists him at 09:00-09:15 as "Paulo Manoel Protasio, Autoridade do Desenvolvimento Sustentavel, ADS - Rio de Janeiro". The words CISBRA and CAERJ appear in none of the six PDFs he sent and in no chat line; "President, CISBRA - CAERJ System" comes only from Pedro's own letter drafts, built from the CISBRA public page (date in the post not confirmed, per the brain-radox SCF drafts). So the letter title must be offered to Paulo as a choice, not stated.
2. **The word "Radox" never appears in the chat export** except in the e-mail address Pedro gave on 16/10. Paulo knows the work as "projeto de tokenizacao Rio <> Miami" and "seu primeiro produto" (16/10). Any text he signs that says "Radox" introduces the name to him in writing.
3. **No Paulo document calls Glocal UpShift a "laboratory"** (text search of all six PDFs; the Glocal deck says "Powered by Rocketbeet", Rocketbeet is the "company intelligence layer"). "The initiative's laboratory for small and medium-sized companies" in the letter drafts is Pedro's wording; it was dropped from the narrowed letter text.
4. **"Part of the ZIS team", "responsible for the technology of the logistics section" and "technology partner of Glocal UpShift" are Pedro's sentences, not Paulo's.** Paulo asked for a draft ("Inclusive um esboco da carta para voce", 23/10 16:20), so the e-mail offers them as Pedro's proposal for Paulo to cut or change, never as something he said.
