# Deck why-now line: DCSA eBL share 1.2 percent in 2021 to about 11 percent in 2025, placed on the problem slide

Decision for the 10-slide YC deck, which has no why-now slide: the why-now is one visible line on slide 2 (Problem), replacing the WTO footer. Wording: ocean bills of lading are going digital, 1.2% in 2021 and about 11% in 2025 (DCSA); the record shared between companies is the missing layer. Why this line: it is a specific, recent, external shift that is already in the verified files ([[dcsa-45-million-bills-of-lading-1af2]]); it describes the market and does not say the rail causes or solves it. Caveat: DCSA's own adoption guide says 5.7% in January 2025 and the newsroom page says about 11% as of 2025, and nobody has reconciled the two ([[dcsa-ebl-5-7-percent-jan-6e39]]); DCSA is a carriers' body that promotes the eBL. The 1.2% and 11% are two pages from one body with different dates, so the line quotes each with its year and draws no growth multiple. Fallback if a judge wants a fresher number: ICC/FIT Alliance Oct 2026, 11.2% of 190 surveyed firms use eBL exclusively ([[icc-fit-alliance-2026-ebl-survey-cdc7]]). The technology half of why-now (one Soroban call needs both companies' signatures, [[two-signers-in-one-soroban-call-1b93]]) is already shown on slides 3 and 6, so it stays out of the line. Not used: the WTO share as why-now, because it is a why-eventually about size, not a recent change ([[wto-80-to-90-percent-of-98f6]]).
