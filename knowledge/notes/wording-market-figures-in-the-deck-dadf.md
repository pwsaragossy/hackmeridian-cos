# Wording market figures in the deck README and form: no pilot, no we start on, one figure per sentence, data year in every claim

A second independent reviewer subagent attacked the ready sentences of MARKET-NUMBERS.md on 2026-10-25 and found an overclaim in all three it fixed. Rules that follow:
1. Never write "pilot corridor" or "we start on" while there is only a testnet demo: write "the corridor we target" or "our design target".
2. Do not turn a single-year figure into a rate: "was about US$94.2 billion in 2025", not "carries about US$94 billion a year".
3. Keep the data year and the qualifier ("goods", "by volume") in every claim. Past tense for a past year.
4. One figure per sentence when they measure different things (value, tonnes, documents). Joining them with "under" or "of it" implies that one contains the other.
5. Do not say our standard "applies to" market totals: that is a claim of reach.
6. A rounded figure on a slide is fine only if the footnote carries the exact figure. An undated source page is footnoted as "undated page, opened <date>".
7. End with "Market figures, not our volumes" and, in the README and form, "we have no pilot or traction yet".
