# Problem slides review 2026-10-25: 22 percent insider figure is faithful, NTC roubo is armed robbery, DCSA 11 and 5.7 clash

[FACT, medium] Reviewer pass on slides 2-3 (2026-10-25). (1) TT Club/BSI PDF p.2 says "Insider involvement accounted for 22% of global cargo theft incidents" (recorded incidents, mostly pilferage at facilities); the Asia section (p.6) separately says 22% of regional thefts, a coincidence a judge may confuse. The same Asia text names custody transfers: "custody transfers, regulatory variations, and documentation changes generate blind spots across ports, cross docks, and rail ramps" (one regional example, not a global stat). (2) NTC via Portal do Transito (28 Mar 2026): "prejuizo direto estimado chega a aproximadamente R$ 900 milhoes"; page title says "roubo de cargas" (robbery, in Brazil usually with violence), no methodology, Southeast 86.8% of cases; a signed handover would not stop armed robbery. (3) DCSA gives two 2025 figures: "5,7% in January 2025" (adoption guide) and "around 11% as of 2025" (newsroom, undated); the 45M/year is a Feb 2023 statement, so "of them electronic in 2025" mixes years. (4) ADB did not investigate drivers; documentation is not a listed rejection reason, so US$2.5T is market size only.
