# HackMeridian 2026 rules and submission requirements (as of 2026-10-25)

Public pages (fetched 2026-10-25 through a summarising fetch tool, so wording is second-hand): up to 5 per team (/faq); AI tools such as Claude Code allowed (/faq, home); you own what you build, but organizers get a non-exclusive licence to show your submission (terms.html sec 06, dated 13 Aug 2026); prize US$30,000 over 6 winners, paid as XLM equivalent (home); prizes non-transferable, winners pay taxes (terms sec 07); no KYC text found on the public pages; deadline Mon 26 Oct 12:00, no late submissions, all times Lisbon local (/schedule), which is UTC+0 per [[lisbon-is-utc-0-for-the-9963]]; judging is async on video, deck, repo and answers; rubric in [[hackmeridian-rubric-7-criteria-weights-scored-50e2]].

Portal tracks PDF (24 Oct): Scale needs an existing Stellar project, scoped new work clearly distinguished from the old, and a deck; every project needs a public GitHub repo, README, architecture overview, demo video or live demo, and an onchain testnet or mainnet transaction; prior work must be disclosed.

Not on any public page and not in the PDF, but in Pedro's verbatim paste of the portal rules (2026-10-24, on disk as projects/hackmeridian-rail/PORTAL-PASTE.md): video 2 to 4 minutes on YouTube or Vimeo, deck up to 20 MiB in PDF/PPTX/PPT/HTML, AI attribution required, disqualification for undisclosed prior work. The same paste says the rubric uses a 1 to 10 scale, against 1 to 5 on /build. The live portal rules page needs login and no agent opened it.

Project editing window in the portal, per Pedro on 2026-10-25 (he has seen the portal): Sun 25 Oct 09:00 to Mon 26 Oct 10:00 Lisbon, so submissions close at 10:00 even though /schedule says hacking ends at 12:00.

Thematic track for Scale, settled by the reviewer reading /build directly: "Thematic tracks do not apply to Scale projects", while the PDF says Scale "can also enter" one, so it is optional and unscored for Scale. Repo content: no rule text requires only new code, and existing code is fine if declared ([[no-text-says-a-hackmeridian-scale-7074]]); the Code of Conduct asks for your own work or work you have the right to use ([[hackmeridian-code-of-conduct-play-fair-4f3e]]). Still open: whether "the project source code" for Scale means the whole pre-existing product, so the closed backend is asked about at check-in.
