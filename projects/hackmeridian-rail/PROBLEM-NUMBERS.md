## Top 2 for the problem

1. Row 1 + row 2 (DCSA). These numbers size the paper chain: 45 million bills of lading a year, and about 9 in 10 still not electronic in 2025.
2. Row 3 (TT Club and BSI, Apr 2026). Insiders and warehouses are the handover points, which is where custody breaks. The data is recent and comes from a named insurer.

Row 4 (ICC, Oct 2026) is the freshest number. It is the fallback if a judge asks for something newer than DCSA's: it shows that even adopters mostly run paper alongside the eBL. Use it as the self-selected survey of 190 records it is.
