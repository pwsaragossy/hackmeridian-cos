## C. What a physical-goods RWA issuer on Stellar needs, and what exists

[FACT from B6, B7] Stellar already carries tokens for physical gold (Matrixdock XAUm: gold in named vault custodians, audited by a third party) and directory projects for agricultural traceability, so a physical-goods issuer can issue a token and prove reserves at rest today. [INFERENCE] The gap is the leg between vaults: while goods move from producer to port to carrier to consignee, the token's backing sits with changing custodians, and the sources above describe vault audits and traceability data but not a record where the outgoing and the incoming custodian each sign the handover. [FACT from A5, A8] The sector's own standards (IMO PCS guidelines, IATA ONE Record) are about connecting systems and sharing data, and the pages I opened do not mention signed custody transfer. What exists for this in our own work: a Soroban contract, proven on testnet only with test keys (see [[two-signers-in-one-soroban-call-1b93]]). Anything beyond that is a goal, not a fact.
