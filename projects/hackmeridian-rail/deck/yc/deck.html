2
Solution

One shared record. Both companies sign every handover.

Warehouse
both sign
Port
both sign
Carrier
both sign
Customs
both sign
Buyer
Both sign

The company giving the goods and the one receiving them authorise the step.

Nobody rewrites

Each step folds into a hash chain. One 32-byte head commits to the whole history.

Anyone checks

A buyer, insurer or lender recomputes the head from public data.

Business model

The SWIFT model: pay to connect, pay per shipment.

Subscription

per connected company

Fee

per shipment on the rail

The shape follows Swift's published billing rules: entry fee, annual charges and usage fees (swift.com, accessed 25 Oct 2026). Radox is not affiliated with Swift.
8
Go-to-market and vision

One corridor first. Then every route.

Now

The rail on testnet and an open spec any Stellar issuer can implement (Apache-2.0).

Next

First live shipment on the US–Brazil corridor with a lender and a logistics operator. Mainnet after an audit.

Then

Every route. Each company joins once, through one adapter to the Radox API.

US–Brazil goods trade was about US$94.2 billion in 2025 (US Census Bureau, exports plus imports).
