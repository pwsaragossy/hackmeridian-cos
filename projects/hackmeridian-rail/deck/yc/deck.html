Every handover signed by both companies, checkable by anyone. Running on Stellar testnet.

None of them can check what the others say about who held the goods.

The money that rides on those records is large: 80–90% of world trade relies on trade finance (WTO).
2
Solution

One shared record. Both companies sign every handover.

Warehouse
both sign
Port
both sign
Carrier
both sign
Customs
both sign
Buyer
Both sign

The company giving the goods and the one receiving them authorise the step.

Nobody rewrites

Each step folds into a hash chain. One 32-byte head commits to the whole history.

Anyone checks

A buyer, insurer or lender recomputes the head from public data.

3
Target users

The companies that hand over goods, and the ones that pay on them.

Buyers, insurers, lenders

Business model

per connected company

Fee
