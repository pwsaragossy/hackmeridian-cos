Stellar Unlocked
Stellar’s full potential is yours to unlock.
Stellar’s protocol is packed with native capabilities: built-in asset-issuance capabilities and a DEX, ZK-ready host functions, and powerful contract features. Everything you need to build meaningful products and services is already in the protocol. The breakthrough is in how you use it. Take Stellar fundamentals further than they’ve gone before, and build something groundbreaking.
Ideas to get you started

* ZK, natively: Use the Poseidon and BN254 host functions to build verifiers, private voting, membership proofs, or a ZK primitive of your own.
* The underrated toolbox: Create trustlines through SACs, or put externally managed contract executables and sparse contract type mappings to work. These powerful features are ready for their breakout project.
* Old-school assets, new tricks: Use Stellar’s built-in asset issuance, DEX and path payments for something they weren’t obviously designed for.
* Protocol-powered games: Build games with real onchain stakes and outcomes on core protocol features.

Submission integrity

* Show your progress during the event. You may start with an existing project, including work begun at a regional activation. Make your starting point and the new work delivered during HackMeridian clear in your README and demo.
* Disclose prior work. Using an existing codebase is fine if you declare it. Undisclosed prior work is a disqualification.
* AI tools are encouraged — attribution is required, not permission. Note in your submission where AI meaningfully generated code or assets.
* Project links and decks. Submit a repository link, a 2–4 minute demo video link (YouTube or Vimeo), and a pitch deck as a PDF, PPTX, PPT, or HTML file up to 20 MiB.

How judging works
Judges review what your team shows at the end of HackMeridian. Your starting point shapes what judges expect to see. How much you had already built, your users and your traction help explain your project, but you still need to show new work from the event.
The rubric. Seven criteria use a 1–10 rating scale. Genesis uses thematic alignment; Scale uses ecosystem alignment:

* Thematic Alignment (Genesis) / Ecosystem Alignment (Scale) — 20%
* Technical Execution — 15%
* User Value & Problem — 20%
* Hackathon Progress — 10%
* Innovation — 15%
* Market & Ecosystem Potential — 10%
* Product Design & UX — 10%

The organizing team will share judging arrangements and any updates with participants during the event.
AI tools may support project assessment. Human judges and organizers make the final decisions.
